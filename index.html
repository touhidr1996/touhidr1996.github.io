01 / Research

Research direction

My research interests connect information retrieval and trustworthy AI. I want to understand when LLM-generated assessments agree with human judgments, where disagreement concentrates, and how evaluation methods can distinguish model errors from uncertainty in the reference judgments.

My master’s work examined a related problem in multi-agent decision making: an LLM can provide context-sensitive reasoning but may cross a formal priority boundary. I investigated those failures experimentally and used a CP-SAT solver to preserve the policy constraints.

03 / Thesis

Master’s thesis

Shizuoka University · 2026

A Study of Decision Making for EV Charging and Situation-Based Routing Using LLMs and CP-SAT Solver in Multi-Agent Systems

Supervisor: Professor Naoki Fukuta

I evaluated whether LLMs followed a lexicographic priority policy in EV charging and routing scenarios. The work examined the effects of explicit policy grounding and a hybrid design in which a solver enforces priority boundaries while an LLM can help order choices within a tier.
